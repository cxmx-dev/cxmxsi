How to play

The pumpkin is a jack-o'-lantern. The moon hangs above it. When the ball goes in the hole, you lose a life. The next ball drops from the moon. Bats fly through the candies and can knock the ball onto a new course. Candies sit between the moon and the pumpkin. A hit candy shouts from its center. Move as the ball lands and that speed is added. Drag, or hold A and D or the arrow keys. A stick or D-pad moves it. On a phone, with the screen facing you, rotate it clockwise or counterclockwise. That works with the phone upright or on its side. A level phone stays put. Tilting farther, up to 15 degrees, moves the pumpkin faster. Tablets stay on touch and buttons. Three lives. Every 6th candy adds a life, up to six. Clear every candy. A confirms. B backs out. Space calls a ghoul hand out of the hole for a short time. The ball bounces off that hand. Start, P, or Esc pauses. M or Y mutes. On a touch screen, Left, Right, Hand, Pause, and Mute sit under the board.
